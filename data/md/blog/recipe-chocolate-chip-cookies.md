Can fit 12 cookies in a **9x13 pan**. Makes 24 cookies total (2 batches).

### Instructions

1. In medium bowl, mix until soft and fluffy (?):

   - **1 cup butter, softened**
   - **1 cup brown sugar**
   - **1/2 to 3/4 cup white sugar**

1. Then mix in:

   - **1 egg**
   - **1 teaspoon vanilla**

1. In a separate large bowl, whisk together:

   - **1 1/2 cups flour**
   - **1/4 teaspoon salt**
   - **1 teaspoon baking soda**
   - **2 cups rolled oats**

1. Fold in medium bowl contents into large bowl. Mix until combined, but not too much.
1. Add in **6 oz chocolate chips**, or however much
1. Set oven to **350°F**.
1. Put dough in fridge until oven is ready. Put balls of dough on parchment paper.
1. Bake for **~12 minutes**, until cookies look good.
