Key is the maple cream cheese frosting. Bake in a **9x13 pan**.

## Instructions

1. Set oven to **350°F**
1. Grease and flour pan

1. Into the food processor, process until carrot pieces are small:

   - **1 lb bag of carrots**
   - **1 cubic inch ginger**

1. Whisk together in large bowl:

   - **1 1/4 cups vegetable oil**
   - **3 eggs**
   - **1 cup white sugar**
   - **1 cup brown sugar**
   - **2 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract**

1. Dry ingredients, mix in a separate large bowl:

   - **2 cups flour**
   - **1/2 teaspoon salt**
   - **2 teaspoons baking powder**
   - **2 teaspoons baking soda**
   - **2 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon**

1. Mix wet and dry ingredients together

1. Mix chopped up carrots into batter

1. Add in **1 cup walnuts** into batter

1. Bake until not wet in the center, about **30 to 35 minutes**

1. Let cake cool in pan for ~5 minutes, then take out of pan to cool on rack. Let cool until not so warm before applying icing.

### Icing

1. Mix in medium bowl until smooth:

   - **8 oz cream cheese** (warm)
   - **1/2 cups butter** (warm)

1. Add in sugar and maple syrup and mix until nice.

   - **~1 cup confectioners sugar**
   - **1/3 cup maple syrup**

Let cool in fridge so it’s not so droopy.
